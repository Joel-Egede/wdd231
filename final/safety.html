Maintenance Safety

Safety

Safe maintenance begins with identifying hazards, controlling energy, and following established workplace procedures.

Before Maintenance

Follow a Safe Process

01

Identify Hazards

Identify electrical, mechanical, thermal, chemical, pressure, and other hazards.

02

Stop Equipment

Stop the equipment using the correct shutdown procedure before beginning maintenance.

03

Control Energy

Isolate hazardous energy and follow applicable lockout/tagout procedures.

04

Verify

Verify that hazardous energy has been controlled before beginning work.

Safety Information

Electrical and Mechanical Safety

Electrical Safety

Electrical work can involve shock, arc flash, burns, and unexpected energization.

  • Identify electrical hazards.
  • Isolate electrical energy.
  • Use appropriate PPE.
  • Verify isolation before work.
  • Follow approved procedures.

Mechanical Safety

Mechanical equipment can contain stored energy and moving parts that can cause serious injury.

  • Identify moving parts.
  • Watch for pinch points.
  • Control stored energy.
  • Keep guards in place.
  • Follow equipment procedures.

Important Safety Reminder

This website provides general educational information. Always follow your employer's safety procedures, equipment manuals, applicable laws, and instructions from qualified personnel.
